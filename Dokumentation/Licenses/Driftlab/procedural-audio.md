# Archived project-created audio

The project inventory and Assets/_Project/Audio/Resources/LIESMICH.txt identify
Audio/Platzhalter_alt as the saved procedural predecessors of the four firearm
recordings. They are not imported recordings and are outside Resources, so they
are not selected by AudioService at runtime. The project's ProceduralSfx.cs
contains the synthesis implementation. No separate redistribution licence for
these generated WAVs is declared by this audit.
